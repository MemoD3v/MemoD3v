About Me

I care about how
things feel.

Good controls, satisfying feedback, readable UI, that's where I start. The polish comes after.

Hey! I'm MemoDev, a game developer, vector artist, and designer from Azerbaijan (UTC +04:00). Right now I'm working at Stopwatch Games Studio on a horror game called The Keyhole, and building my own thing called Lightbreak on the side.

I started out just making little experiments and game jam entries, and somehow turned that into shipping games across Unity, Löve2D, Roblox, and the browser. I genuinely failed 8th grade math, which makes it pretty funny that I now spend my days writing game systems for a living.

What I really care about is making stuff that feels right. A platformer that's satisfying to move in. A shader that catches your eye. A UI that doesn't get in the way. I'll spend way too long tweaking things most people won't even notice, and I'm totally okay with that.

If you want to chat, find me on Discord as memorysilver, my DMs are open.
